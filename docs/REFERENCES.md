# Theoretical References

The following works ground the six methodologies of this framework in established research traditions.

---

## Constructivism

- Piaget, J. (1952). *The Origins of Intelligence in Children*. International Universities Press.
- Vygotsky, L. S. (1978). *Mind in Society: The Development of Higher Psychological Processes*. Harvard University Press.

**Applied in:** MSCM (M1), GIT (M3)

---

## Cognitive Load Theory

- Sweller, J. (1988). Cognitive load during problem solving: Effects on learning. *Cognitive Science, 12*(2), 257–285.

**Applied in:** SWEM (M2)

---

## Dual Coding Theory

- Paivio, A. (1971). *Imagery and Verbal Processes*. Holt, Rinehart & Winston.

**Applied in:** MSCM (M1)

---

## Gradual Release of Responsibility

- Pearson, P. D., & Gallagher, M. C. (1983). The instruction of reading comprehension. *Contemporary Educational Psychology, 8*(3), 317–344.

**Applied in:** GIT (M3)

---

## Elaborative Interrogation

- Pressley, M., Wood, E., Woloshyn, V. E., Martin, V., King, A., & Menke, D. (1992). Encouraging mindful use of prior knowledge: Attempting to construct explanatory answers facilitates learning. *Educational Psychologist, 27*(1), 91–109.

**Applied in:** VCE (M4)

---

## Situated Cognition

- Brown, J. S., Collins, A., & Duguid, P. (1989). Situated cognition and the culture of learning. *Educational Researcher, 18*(1), 32–42.

**Applied in:** CM (M6)
