Section 2 — Core Instructional Cycle

The Seven-Phase Teaching Sequence

A replicable lesson structure synthesising all six methodologies. Phases 4–6 constitute an inner loop that repeats for each new worked example within a session.

Phase 01

Pre-Experience (Tactile)

Touch, fold paper, physically partition objects into equal parts to build haptic schema

Phase 02

Verbal Description

Student narrates the tactile experience; instructor guides precise mathematical language

Phase 03

Generalisation

Tactile experience is abstracted into formal mathematical notation and symbolic representation

Phase 04 ↻

Worked Example

Instructor solves with full verbal narration of procedure and underlying rationale

Phase 05 ↻

Guided Practice

Student attempts a parallel problem with instructor present; corrective feedback offered

Phase 06 ↻  ★

Student Explanation

Student reconstructs and verbalises the full solution; most critical phase for consolidation

Phase 07

Multimedia Reinforcement

Optional: one curated audio/video resource; student restates its key content verbally

Inner loop: Phases 4 → 5 → 6 repeat for each new worked example within a single session before progressing. Phase 6 (Student Explanation) is non-negotiable and must not be abbreviated or skipped.
